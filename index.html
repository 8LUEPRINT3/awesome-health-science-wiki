    Textbooks

    Sorrentino's Canadian Textbook for the Support Worker

    Mary J. Wilk, Sheila A. Sorrentino, Leighann N. Remmert — Elsevier, 5th Canadian Edition (2022)

    The comprehensive guide for personal support workers in Canada. Covers client-centred care, safety, communication, and clinical skills.

    Part Topics
    Part 1 Role & Responsibilities, Canadian Health Care System, Workplace Settings, Ethics, Legislation
    Part 2 Health & Wellness, Culture & Diversity, Stress Management, Communication, Teamwork
    Part 3 Clients & Families, Abuse Awareness, Career Start, Body Mechanics, Exercise & Activity
    Part 4 Rehabilitation, Personal Hygiene, Grooming, Infection Prevention, Safety
    Part 5 Beds & Bed Making, Skin Care, Comfort & Pain, Vital Signs, Medical Terminology
    Part 6 Care Planning, Nutrition, Enteral Nutrition, Urinary Elimination, Bowel Elimination
    Part 7 Growth & Development, Body Structure & Function, Diseases & Disorders
    Part 8 Hearing & Vision, Speech & Language, Developmental Disorders, Mental Health
    Part 9 Dementia, Oxygenation, Heat & Cold, Infant & Child Care, Older Persons
    Part 10 Home Management, Medication Management, Acute Care, End-of-Life Care
    Part 11 Appendix, References, Procedures
    Part 12-19 Additional chapters and procedures

    Key Concepts: DIPPS (Dignity, Independence, Individualized care, Preferences, Privacy, Safety), scope of practice, client-centred care, infection control, body mechanics, vital signs, care planning.

    The Human Body in Health and Illness

    Barbara Herlihy — Elsevier, 7th Edition (2022)

    Anatomy and physiology textbook with clinical correlations. Structure meets function with pathophysiology integrated throughout.

    Part Topics
    Part 1 Introduction to the Human Body, Levels of Organization, Organ Systems, Homeostasis, Anatomical Terms
    Part 2 Basic Chemistry, Cells, Cell Metabolism
    Part 3 Microbiology Basics, Tissues and Membranes
    Part 4 Integumentary System, Body Temperature, Skeletal System
    Part 5 Muscular System, Nervous System (Brain & Nervous Tissue)
    Part 6 Spinal Cord, Peripheral Nerves, Autonomic Nervous System, Sensory System
    Part 7 Endocrine System, Blood
    Part 8 Heart Anatomy & Function, Blood Vessels, Circulation
    Part 9 Lymphatic System, Immune System, Respiratory System
    Part 10 Digestive System, Urinary System
    Part 11 Water, Electrolyte & Acid-Base Balance, Reproductive Systems
    Part 12 Human Development and Heredity
    Part 13 Answers, Glossary

    Key Concepts: Homeostasis, structure-function relationships, 12 organ systems, anatomical terminology, pathophysiology, clinical correlations.

    Integrating a Palliative Approach

    Katherine Murray — Life and Death Matters, 2nd Edition

    Essentials for personal support workers providing palliative and end-of-life care.

    Part Topics
    Part 1 Understanding Dying, Palliative Approach, Preparing to Care
    Part 2 Standardized Tools, Supporting Physical Comfort
    Part 3 Psychosocial Care, Last Days and Hours
    Part 4 Self-Care for Caregivers, Grief, Cultural Safety

    Key Concepts: Palliative approach, person-centred care, self-awareness, cultural safety, psychosocial support, end-of-life care, caregiver self-care.

    How to Use

    1. Browse by textbook — Each book is organized into logical parts
    2. Search — Use GitHub's search (press /) to find specific topics across all files
    3. Cross-reference — Concepts link between books (e.g., body mechanics in Sorrentino's connects to musculoskeletal system in The Human Body)
    4. Study — Each part contains chapter summaries, key terms, and clinical correlations

    Local Setup & Obsidian Integration

    To use this knowledge base locally with full graph visualization and instant search:

    1. Clone the repository: 

      git clone https://github.com/8LUEPRINT3/awesome-health-science-wiki.git

    2. Open in Obsidian:

    3. Launch Obsidian
    4. Click Open folder as vault

    5. Select the cloned awesome-health-science-wiki folder

    6. Explore:

    7. Use the Graph View to visualize connections between topics
    8. Use Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + F for full-text search across all textbooks
    9. Use Ctrl/Cmd + O to quickly jump to any file
